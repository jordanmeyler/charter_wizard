# The language

A spell is a **chain of concepts**. It should read as a sentence. A short chain is a simple sentence. A long chain is a story with a turn. Complicated work uses more runes because more happened.

World substances — what you stamp on a tile — live beside this book in [`MATERIALS.md`](MATERIALS.md). A material is a sentence the world has already become.

Players will eventually see **effects, not names**. If you only watch what the world does, the chain has to be the only explanation that fits.

Hot, Cold, Wet, Dry, and Aether stay reserved. **Anima** and **Animus** are wrought now — eros and logos, born with Sulphur. Male and Female are the old names for those two.

There is no hit-point bar. A spell **kills**, **restrains**, or does **neither**. Kill is binary: `power × affinity > defense`. Affinity is 0–5. Defense is 0–10. Power is 0–10. Witchfire is 7 and nothing resists it. Glacier sent is 6. Unmake is 10, elementless chaos. Ordinary air is force **1–2** and still **pushes**; a later tempest can hit harder. Air ward / gale-form refuse the shove.

Formation (Shot, Pillar, Spread, Remote, Self) is **written in the chain**, not chosen later. Earth at the end of a body stands (Pillar). Mercury without breath goes *into* a thing or lands away (Remote). Breath already in the chain, then Mercury, flies (Shot). A body around the feet is Spread. Hop, Blink, Teleport, and Flight stay on the caster (Self). A pillar is one tile on the floor: a hollow takes it and holds; a floor grows a column. A wall asks for a start and a stop — across a pit it is a two-tile span, on the floor it is a barrier. Standard earth and ice must find a floor or a wall at each end, or the span falls. Ice freezes water without banks. Earth only muds water. A wood wall is a line of trees — over a pit it needs the same banks; on water it grows a walkable cover without banks, the way ice freezes a pool. Hunger eats it. Metal hangs without a far rest. Columns follow the same law.

## 1. The eleven basic runes

These are the only runes you can write from nothing. Everything else is born when they meet.

Each one is a **concept**. It is not a stat, a damage type, or a UI verb.

### Roots — kinds of matter

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire** | Hunger. The will to consume so it can continue. | Things eaten, brightened, dried, ended as wet |
| **Air** | Breath. The between. That which has no weight and will not stay. | Lift, space, a thing that travels *through* rather than *into* |
| **Earth** | Rest. Patience. That which remains when everything else has left. | Weight, stay, ground, a thing that will not be hurried |
| **Water** | Yield. Mercy. That which becomes what holds it. | Wet, give, wash, a thing that takes a vessel’s shape |

### Operators — what you do to a concept

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Salt** | A standing body. Manifestation that stays. | Walls, pillars, a floor that holds. Salt is how a work *stands*. It is not how a spark flies |
| **Mercury** | Going. A path opening. | Something that travels. Hunger sent is fire. Breath then Mercury is a bolt. A stood body then Mercury goes *into* a thing |
| **Sulphur** | The wildcard. A turn that makes the work into something else. | Like Life makes a plant living: add Sulphur and the same sentence is no longer the same spell |

### Veils — shown and withheld

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** | Shown. The veil is lifted. | What was hidden can be seen |
| **Dark** | Withheld. The veil is drawn. | Hidden, called away, or refused breath |

### Modifiers — living and the grave

These are not everyday operators. They colour a chain that is already about a being, or they open work the Charter does not like to write.

| Rune | Concept | How it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Life** | Waking. The thing keeps being itself. | **Part of the recipe for a living thing.** A plant, a hop, a heal, a called beast, a homunculus. Ice, stone, fire, wind, and shades do not carry it. |
| **Death** | The grave. The motion leaves and does not return. | **Reserved.** Not in standard recipes. It does work in line with death, and it is a load-bearing rune for **Free** magic. Charter may fizzle the worst of it. |

That is the whole alphabet: **Fire, Air, Earth, Water, Salt, Mercury, Sulphur, Light, Dark, Life, Death.**

## 2. How a chain is read (without names)

Left to right, like a sentence. **The order a spell is cast — and the order runes are shown — is the sentence.** A later rune happens to what came before. Charter wants that order. Free may unscramble the same runes into a written recipe.

1. **Two roots meeting become a third.** You do not see “fire and air.” You see a seed of charge. That seed is **Spark**, and it is its own rune.
2. **Salt** makes a work *stand* — a wall, a pillar, a floor-body. Salt is manifestation, not shooting.
3. **Mercury** makes it go. Hunger sent is fire (**Fire · Mercury**). Hunger given breath, then sent, is a sting (**Fire · Air · Mercury** — Spark shot). The seed stretched through more breath, then sent, is a bolt (**Fire · Air · Air · Mercury** — Lightning bolt). A stood fire-body sent *into* a thing is melt (**Fire · Salt · Mercury**).
4. **Light** shows. **Dark** withholds.
5. **Sulphur** is the wildcard. Add it and the recipe becomes a different work — the way Life makes a plant living.
6. **Life** is only there if the subject or the product is living. It is a mark on a living recipe, not a way to “add life magic.”
7. **Death** is not in the ordinary book. If you see it, the work is grave-work.
8. **Repeating a concept intensifies it.** Air · Air is more breath.
9. **Two runes** may birth a join, wait as a clause, or finish a short spell. **Fire · Mercury** is fire. Longer is a rarer working. Hop, Blink, Teleport, and Flight use the same ideas in a different order.

Example, watched only as an effect:

> Hunger flies. Water thrown on it ends it.

Hunger was sent (**Mercury**). **Fire · Mercury**. That is fire. No Salt — a hunting flame is not a wall.

**Fire · Air · Mercury** is a different story. Hunger learned breath (**Spark**) and was sent. That is a sting — **Spark shot**. If Spark already stands: **Spark · Mercury**. Stretch the seed through more breath and send it (**Lightning · Mercury**, or **Fire · Air · Air · Mercury**) and that is **Lightning bolt**.

**Fire · Salt** is hunger given a standing body. A fire-pillar. Without a source it goes out in a few seconds. **Fire · Salt · Earth** asks that hunger to rest — a lasting hearth column. **Fire · Earth · Salt** is hungry earth given a body: a lava-pillar.

**Fire · Salt · Mercury** is that stood body sent *into* a thing. Melt, not fire. It bores stone and steel walls — even the masonry of the room. Obsidian will not take it.

**Fire · Air · Sulphur · Mercury** is the wildcard on the bolt. The same moving spark now reaches a mind. That is Jolt, not fire.

**Ice** is yield meeting rest: **Water · Earth**. It melts. **Water · Earth · Salt** is a water-pillar. **Water · Salt · Earth** is Plant. The grim twin (**Water · Salt · Death**) is grave-ice, and it is not in the ordinary book.

## 3. Wrought runes

A join is not a modifier. It is a **new rune** with its own concept. Once it exists (you mixed it, or the room already holds it), later chains can start from it.

Most joins are elements meeting elements (**Steam · Metal → Acid**). Ice is **Water · Earth**. Water-pillar is **Water · Earth · Salt**. The Grimoire lists every written birth, split that way.

## 7. Why this split

**Life** is not a school. It is the mark that the recipe is *about a living thing*. Sprout without Life is a seed. Hop is breath given a body (Air · Salt · Air) and does not need Life. Blink, Teleport, and Flight write more after that same hop. Float is breath going, then stood (`Air · Mercury · Salt`) — you hang; wind and vine move you. It is the other order, not a hop expansion. Call without Life is a statue.

**Salt** is not a school either. It is how a work *stands*: walls, pillars, a live floor, a wick, ice that holds. A hunting spark does not need it.

**Sulphur** is the wildcard, the way Life is the living mark. Fire plus Sulphur is Rage. Lightning plus Sulphur is Jolt. Ignite is hunger wildcarded and then stood (Salt) as a wick.

**Death** is not how you freeze water or open a pit. Rest already does that. Death is how Free reaches the grave — blight, shade, unmaking, the sleep that is not sleep, ice that will not thaw. The ordinary book should read as if Death had never been found.
