# The language

A spell is a **chain of concepts**. It should read as a sentence. A short chain is a simple sentence. A long chain is a story with a turn. Complicated work uses more runes because more happened.

Players will eventually see **effects, not names**. If you only watch what the world does, the chain has to be the only explanation that fits.

Primordial runes (Hot, Cold, Wet, Dry, Aether, Animus, Anima) are out of this pass.

There is no damage. A spell **kills**, **restrains**, or does **neither**.

**Life** only when the subject or the product is living. Ice, stone, fire, wind, and shades do not carry it.

Formation (Shot, Pillar, Spread, Remote, Self) is **how you place** a finished chain. It is not a rune.

---

## 1. The eleven basic runes

These are the only runes you can write from nothing. Everything else is born when they meet.

Each one is a **concept**. It is not a stat, a damage type, or a UI verb.

### Roots — kinds of matter

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire** | Hunger. The will to consume so it can continue. | Things eaten, brightened, dried, ended as wet |
| **Air** | Breath. The between. That which has no weight and will not stay. | Lift, space, a thing that travels *through* rather than *into* |
| **Earth** | Rest. Patience. That which remains when everything else has left. | Weight, stay, ground, a thing that will not be hurried |
| **Water** | Yield. Mercy. That which becomes what holds it. | Wet, give, wash, a thing that takes a vessel’s shape |

### Operators — what you do to a concept

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Salt** | A body. “This, here, is a thing.” Coherence. | Something you can point at. Without Salt the work is weather or a quality |
| **Mercury** | Going. A path opening. | Something that travels. Without Mercury the work stays where it was born |
| **Sulphur** | Passion. A mind that can be reached. | Something that decides, erupts, or feels. Without Sulphur nothing thinks |

### Poles — waking and still

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Life** | Waking. The thing keeps being itself. Growth. | A thing that hops, heals, grows, or is called alive. Never on ice, stone, fire, wind, shade |
| **Death** | Still. The motion leaves the thing. Not moral. | A thing that stops, freezes, snuffs, sleeps, or comes apart |

### Veils — shown and withheld

| Rune | Concept | What you see if you do not know the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** | Shown. The veil is lifted. | What was hidden can be seen |
| **Dark** | Withheld. The veil is drawn. | What was seen is kept back — hidden, called away, or refused breath |

That is the whole alphabet: **Fire, Air, Earth, Water, Salt, Mercury, Sulphur, Life, Death, Light, Dark.**

## 2. How a chain is read (without names)

Left to right, like a sentence. A later rune happens to what came before.

1. **Two roots meeting become a third.** You do not see “fire and air.” You see a seed of charge. That seed is **Spark**, and it is its own rune.
2. **Salt** makes a body — something you can point at.
3. **Mercury** makes it go. If breath is already in the chain (Air, Spark, Lightning), it travels *through space*. If not, it goes *into* the thing you aim at.
4. **Death** stills. **Life** wakes. Life only if the subject or product lives.
5. **Light** shows. **Dark** withholds.
6. **Sulphur** is a mind being moved.
7. **Repeating a concept intensifies it.** Air · Air is more breath.
8. **Two runes** birth a join, or wait as a clause. **Three or more** is a finished spell. Longer is a rarer working.

Example, watched only as an effect:

> A seed of heat jumps. It is a thing you can point at. It flies. Water thrown on it ends it.

The chain that fits: hunger learned breath (**Spark**), was given a body (**Salt**), and was sent (**Mercury**). Written from the eleven: **Fire · Air · Salt · Mercury**. If Spark is already in the field: **Spark · Salt · Mercury**.

**Fire · Salt · Mercury** is a different story. There is no breath, so the fire-body does not fly — it goes *into* the thing you touch. That is melt, not a hunting spark.

## 3. Wrought runes

A join is not a modifier. It is a **new rune** with its own concept. Once it exists (you mixed it, or the room already holds it), later chains can start from it.

You may always write the long form from the eleven. The wrought name is the same story, folded.

### First joins — two roots

| Wrought | Born from | Concept | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Spark** | Fire · Air | Hunger given breath. A seed of charge. | A sting, a jump of heat, the start of an arc |
| **Steam** | Fire · Water | Hunger forced through yield. Water that cannot stay water. | A violent hot veil |
| **Lava** | Fire · Earth | Hunger meeting rest. Earth that cannot stay earth. | Stone that still hungers |
| **Cloud** | Air · Water | Breath holding yield. A hanging veil. | Weather waiting, a wet sky |
| **Dust** | Air · Earth | Breath forced through rest. Rest that has lost its weight. | Blind grit, a thing that will not sit |
| **Mud** | Water · Earth | Yield meeting rest. Soft ground. | Ground that gives |

### From Spark and Cloud

| Wrought | Born from | Concept | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lightning** | Spark · Air | The seed stretched through more breath. The arc itself. | A bolt. Not a body — a path |
| **Storm** | Spark · Cloud | The seed inside the hanging veil. | Thunder waiting in weather |
| **Thunder** | Lightning · Earth | The arc meeting rest. | Shock in the ground, a clap |
| **Rain** | Cloud · Earth | The hanging veil drawn down. | Wet from above. Fire drowns |
| **Fog** | Cloud · Dark | The hanging veil withheld from sight. | The room is lost |

### Bodies, stills, and vegetable matter

| Wrought | Born from | Concept | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flame** | Fire · Salt | Hunger given a body. Standing fire. | A torch, a column, a hearth |
| **Ember** | Fire · Death | Hunger after the motion left. | Heat without a flame |
| **Ice** | Water · Salt · Death | Yield given a body, then stilled. | Solid water. Not living |
| **Stone** | Earth · Salt · Death | Rest given a body, then stilled. | Hard mass. Not living |

| **Grove** | Plant · Life | The vegetable body waking. | Growth, vine, thicket |
| **Ash** | Flame · Death | A fire-body after the motion left. | What hunger leaves |
| **Sand** | Dust · Salt | Grit given a body. | Grains you can point at |
| **Glass** | Sand · Flame · Death | Grains, a fire-body, then still. | They remember being liquid |
| **Obsidian** | Lava · Water · Salt | Hungry earth quenched and given a body. | Black glass. A path over the hazard |
| **Shade** | Dark · Death · Salt | Withheld, stilled, given a body. No waking. | A shape that is not alive |
| **Blight** | Grove · Death | Waking plant, then stilled. | Verdure that rots. No soul |

### Verbs that have become things

These are still wrought — a root taught to go, named as a thing.

| Wrought | Born from | Concept | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wind** | Air · Mercury | Breath going. | A push, a path of air |
| **Current** | Water · Mercury | Yield going. | A flow, a source |
| **Quake** | Earth · Mercury | Rest asked to go. | The ground moves |

Two stories may arrive at the same wrought rune. **Stone** is rest given a body and stilled (**Earth · Salt · Death**), or hungry earth stilled (**Lava · Death**). Same concept, different history.

- Hound → Earth · Water · Salt · Life · Mercury

Without Life you get a statue or a corpse (**Salt · Water · Earth · Fire · Death**, then Mercury if it must arrive). Without the formula, it fizzles. Charter will not touch a soul.

## 5. Why the old short pairs were wrong

**Fire · Mercury** is not a fireball. It is hunger, going — into whatever you aim at, because there is no breath and no body. That is a clause, or the start of melt if you add Salt.

**Fire · Air · Mercury** is Spark sent, still not a body. That is closer to a raw sting than a hunting spark. Add Salt and it becomes Fireball. Stretch the breath (**Air** again) and it becomes Lightning.

**Water · Death** stills a pool but does not make ice. Ice needs a body first (**Salt**), then the stilling. Otherwise the water stops and stays water.

**Water · Earth** is mud, not a plant. A plant is wet rest given a body (**Salt**). A living plant then wakes (**Life**). Blight names that waking and stills it.

Mind-work can stay short. **Fire · Sulphur · Mercury** is a complete sentence: hunger's passion, sent. The story has no turn. Flight and Chain and Homunculus are longer because more happened.

## 6. The sanctum slice

The playable rooms still resolve a **compressed** grammar (a folded material, a last operator, a formation) so the four locks can be taught with short strings. The book above is the real language. The composer should learn to fold joins the way this document does — that work is next, not this pass.
