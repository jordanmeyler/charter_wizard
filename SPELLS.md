# Spell Codex — draft for review

*Companion to `DESIGN.md`. Names provisional. The test for every recipe is alchemical sense plus taste, not a full grid. Families mix runes in different ways. There is no damage: a spell **kills**, **restrains**, or does **neither** (traverse, transform, heal, hide, lift, summon, reveal).*

Formation (Shot / Pillar / Spread / Remote) is how you *place* a written spell. It is not a third rune. Some spells only make sense in one formation; some refuse formation entirely (self-body, stance).

---

## 1. Primary runes

These are the alphabet. Everything else is mixed from them.

### Substance — the four roots

| Rune | Qualities | As a component of matter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire** | Hot · Dry | Consumption, heat, light-seed, ending of wet things |
| **Air** | Hot · Wet | Mobility, breath, lift, dispersal, weather-seed |
| **Earth** | Cold · Dry | Mass, rest, mineral, the hard chill |
| **Water** | Cold · Wet | Yield, flow, solvent, the soft chill |

They are not “pure elements you throw.” They are what matter is *made of*. A plant is not “the plant element.” It is wet earth given a body.

### Tria prima — body, motion, mind

| Rune | Principle | What it does to a substance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Salt** | Body / Matter | Fixes, structures, lasts, sits on the self. Walls, ice, flesh, wood, a cloak. |
| **Mercury** | Motion / Spirit | Flows, flies, travels, loosens. Bolts, jets, wind, a jump. |
| **Sulphur** | Mind / Passion | Erupts, ignites, reaches a mind. Fear, rage, sleep, command, explosion. |

### Existential

| Rune | Pole | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Life** (Vita) | Animating | Grows, heals, wakes, makes soulless life legal |
| **Death** (Mors) | Stilling | Snuffs, rots, ends, stills. Not moral. Policed by whether a soul is touched. |

### Light / Dark and soul (divine layer)

| Rune | Pole | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** (Lumen) | Sol · projective | Reveals, burns clean, awe, day |
| **Dark** (Umbra) | Luna · receptive | Hides, swallows, night, weight without mass |
| **Animus** | Projective soul | Drive, going-out, force of will |
| **Anima** | Receptive soul | Draw, taking-in, welcome, healing-receive |

Charter cannot *touch* a soul. Animus / Anima as colour on matter (drive, draw) can be legal. Binding, extracting, or inserting a soul is Free or divine.

### Catalyst

| Rune | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Aether** | Prima materia. Inert alone. Opens true generation, high unions, philosopher-stone work. Needed when you make a thing that was not implied by the matter. |

### Mundane primordials — quality shifters

| Rune | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Hot** | Nudge toward Fire / Air |
| **Cold** | Nudge toward Earth / Water. Ice without waiting for winter. |
| **Wet** | Nudge toward Air / Water |
| **Dry** | Nudge toward Fire / Earth. A wick, a desert plant. |

Knowledge-gated. Synthesizable. Not a substitute for an aspect on their own.

## 2. How families mix (this is the grammar)

There is **no** single “material × aspect = spell” law that every family obeys. Each family has its own habit.

| Family | How it mixes | Typical outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Energy** | Substance + Mercury (it travels) or Sulphur (it erupts) or Death (it ends). Hot blends climb: Fire → Spark → Lightning → Storm → Plasma. | Kill, or Neither (light a wick, snuff, melt) |
| **Weather / Air** | Air is the seed. Mercury makes wind. Cloud/Rain/Snow are *substances* you then place. Lift of the **self** is Air + Mercury + Salt (your body). True flight adds Life or Aether. | Neither (jump, fly, douse, obscure), Restrain (blow back, white-out) |
| **Water / Ice** | Water + Salt fixes ice. Earth in the string is the hard chill, not “dirt.” Flood is Water + Mercury. Sleep is Water + Sulphur. | Restrain, Neither (bridge, douse, heal-well) |
| **Stone / Metal** | Earth + Salt **is** Stone. Lava is Fire + Earth, then quench or smelt. These are terrain first. Crush-kill is rare and needs Death or mass + motion. | Neither (bridge, wall, path), Restrain (pin, bog) |
| **Life** | **Plant = Water · Earth · Salt.** Wet earth given a body. Add Life to make it grow. Add Aether when you generate life that was not already implied. Specific plants add a colour (Death → blight, Dry → thorn, Fire → pepper, Dark → nightshade). | Neither (grow, climb, heal), Restrain (vine), Kill only as blight / ash |
| **Mind** | Sulphur is the stem. The substance *colours* the mood: Fire-rage, Water-sleep, Earth-dread, Air-daze, Dark-terror, Light-awe. Animus/Anima turn colour into command (Charter-legal only if no soul is bound). | Restrain, or Neither (rage as a key: they break their own ward) |
| **Light / Dark** | Light rides Fire or Air. Dark rides Earth or Aether. Dark matter is not “more Dark” — it is Dark given Body and Death and Aether. | Kill (swallow), Restrain (well), Neither (hide, reveal) |
| **Manifest** | Two knowledge gates: (1) the **class runes** that make that kind of creature legal, (2) the **creature’s own formula**. Missing either, it fizzles. Charter: soulless only. | Neither (an actor appears) |

**Plant vs Ice** (same three runes would collide):

| Substance | Recipe | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plant** | Water · Earth · Salt | Wet earth given a body. Stem, leaf, wood. Your reading. |
| **Ice** | Water · Salt · Cold | Body of water, quality-shifted. |
| **Ice** (long form, no Cold) | Water · Earth · Salt · Dry? No. Water · Salt, and Earth’s cold is borrowed only if Cold is not known — *or* you accept the Liber Materia line Water · Earth · Salt as Ice and write Plant as Water · Earth · Salt · **Life**. |

**Ratify one.** Recommendation: **Plant = Water · Earth · Salt.** **Ice = Water · Salt · Cold** (long: Water · Earth · Salt when you are working in the Water family and mean “hard chill,” which is a learned reading, not an automatic fold). Living growth is Plant · Life. Codex “Mud · Life · Aether” becomes **true soulless life** — a plant that can be a creature lock.

## 6. What is playable in the sanctum *now*

The four rooms still teach the short strings. This codex is the map of where the tree goes. The Grimoire lists this catalog for review; only a subset resolves locks today.

Ratify next:

1. Plant = Water · Earth · Salt, Ice = Water · Salt · Cold — yes/no.
2. Flight = Air · Mercury · Life (Charter) vs True flight needing Aether — yes/no.
3. Dark matter = Dark · Death · Salt · Aether (Kill, Divine) vs Event well as the Restrain prelude — yes/no.
4. Manifestation always needs class + formula, generic homunculus excepted — yes/no.
5. Rage as Neither (a key that makes them act) rather than Restrain — yes/no.
6. Which names to print on the wall: Life/Death/Light/Dark or Vita/Mors/Lumen/Umbra.
